# Coverage Study

A 50-item Asia sample covered tier 4 through 8 bags, capes, and common resources across the six-city set. At a two-hour age limit and 30-minute skew limit, the sample produced:

```text
Items scanned: 50
Items with at least two both-side usable markets: 0
Items with all six usable markets: 0
Median both-side usable coverage: 0%
Gross opportunities: 0
```

The API returned records for every requested item/city combination, but most quote sides were not fresh. Record availability is therefore not the same as actionable side coverage.

The scanner records fresh bids, fresh asks, bid-only markets, ask-only markets, both-side markets, and potential cross-market pairs. Both-side health is useful, but an arbitrage leg only needs a fresh ask somewhere and a fresh bid elsewhere.
